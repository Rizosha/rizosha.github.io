# About Me


As a dedicated Game Developer, I have graduated from the University of Salford with a 1st class in Games design
and production. I specialize in programming and have had plenty experience working within Unity and Unreal Engine 5 for 
the past 3 years, creating an array of projects, both solo and in collaborative teams, each providing the opportunity to
navigate all stages of game development from Pitch and Prototype through to Development, Testing, and Production.

After being directly involved and learning more about game development, I have fallen in love with the problem-solving 
aspect of bringing concepts and ideas to life through coding building project. I have a constant desire to improve 
myself and have spent a great amount of time on self-study through creating a variety of projects for fun and personal
development.
