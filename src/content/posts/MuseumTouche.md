# Overview

This was a group submission for the Brackeys Game Jam 2025.1 with a theme of Nothing Can Go Wrong. In this game you take over 4 weapon cases and try to prevent museum goers from touching you. If you fail, the cases will eventually crumble to dust. 

# Controls

Press Spacebar to attack
